MFRL Framework

↓
Multi-temperature Sampling + Rejection Filter
↓
Candidates: {y₁, y₂, y₃, y₄}
↓
MFC
Multi-form Feedback Collector
Rule Feedback (ROUGE)
Self-Reward (LogP)
↓
s_RF + s_MF
↓
AFF
Adaptive Feedback Fusion
Attention Weights
Score Normalization
↓
Preference Pairs: (x, y+, y-)
↓
POF
Preference Optimization Fine-tuning
DPO Loss
LoRA (rank=8)
↓
Output: Optimized Model πθ
